# Review desk design

Mode: Operate. A small review workbench rather than a marketing page.

Use a warm, light reading surface for a household task reviewed at a desk or on a phone. A compact task list sits beside a generous reading column on wide screens. On a phone, selecting a task opens the detail with a clear route back. This is a scoped reference design, not a new identity for the broader program.

## Tokens

Canvas #f5f3ed; paper #fffefa; ink #243b32; muted ink #54665b; rule #d8ded4; accent #285b45; accent wash #e5eee3; caution #785014 on #fcf1dc; error #923c2f on #fff0eb.

Body uses the native UI sans stack. Task headings use Georgia with a serif fallback as a reading distinction, not a display brand. Main text stays within 68ch. Controls are at least 44px high. Separators and space carry grouping; do not nest cards or use metric tiles. Small status markers describe actual workflow states.

The authored interaction is selection moving focus from the list to the task, followed by a deliberate decision panel. Motion is brief and respects reduced-motion preferences. No hidden entrance content, gradients, decoration, remote fonts or images.

## First viewport

Household review, a persistent example-mode indicator, the task queue, and the selected furnace-baseline suggestion. Missing evidence is readable before the decision controls. No action is preselected; a human supplies the decision record and outcome record when required.

## States

Loading, empty, failure, missing details, partial coverage, stale version, uncertain decision result, example-only decision and successful connected decision are distinct. Source references are selectable text, never executable links.
